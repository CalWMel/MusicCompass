**8. Additional Comments / Observations (Dissertation Gold!)**
* **The "Winter Gloves" Use Case:** P12 pointed out a brilliant accessibility and contextual use case—using the app when wearing thick gloves (where touching a screen precisely is impossible). A spatial/gesture-based interface completely bypasses the need for fine-motor touch controls.
* **The "Rolodex / Grid" Metaphor:** P12 noted that spinning 360 degrees doesn't work in situations where you can't physically move your body (e.g., on a crowded train or bus). They suggested an incredible alternative mapping: Instead of a compass around the user, map the audio to a "grid" or a "rolodex" directly in front of the user. The user could then use the phone's gyroscope to "flick" left/right or up/down through the tracks without ever having to rotate their torso. 
*(Note for Diss: This perfectly synthesizes the feedback you got from P3, P6, and P8 about "flicking" and "switches." It proves that while the spatial compass is cool, a linear "Rolodex" spatial metaphor might be far more practical for everyday mobile use!)*
