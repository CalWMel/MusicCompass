**8. Additional Comments / Observations (Dissertation Gold!)**
* **The "Moving Vehicle" Gyroscope Problem:** P9 made a brilliant point about using the interface *on* the bus. If the bus physically turns a corner, the gyroscope will register a movement, completely messing up the user's spatial target even if the user stood completely still. *(Note for Diss: This is a **massive** point for your "Context of Use" analysis. It proves that spatial continuous mappings are vulnerable to environmental movement, and perfectly justifies why users might prefer a discrete "flick" gesture instead!)*
* **Sensitivity Issues:** P9 noted that the mapping sometimes felt inconsistent: "I felt like I was turning like a full 90° and it wasn't changing genres, whereas other times I was skipping past genres really quickly." They warned this could get frustrating in everyday use outside of a lab environment.
